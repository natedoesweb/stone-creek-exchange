Lapis Place Nursery is a community-supported neighborhood nursery located on Lapis Place in Bend, Oregon. We grow healthy, locally adapted vegetable, herb, and flower starts for our neighbors and the local community.


Every plant includes a QR code that links back to this page, where you'll find the latest plant availability, growing tips, seasonal updates, and information about our community-supported nursery. As the nursery grows, this page will continue to serve as the central hub for everything we're growing.

## Community-Supported Pricing

Lapis Place Nursery operates on a community-supported pricing model. Each plant has a suggested contribution based on the time and materials required to grow it, but there are no fixed prices. If you're able to contribute a little more, your generosity helps provide supplies and seeds for future growing seasons. If money is tight, you're still welcome to take home healthy plants and start a garden.

**Suggested contributions:**

- Basil: $4
- Oregano: $4
- Jalapeño Peppers: $5
- Beefsteak Tomatoes: $5–6
- Walla Walla Onions: $4
- Strawflowers: $4–5
- Lavender: $6–8

Plants can be purchased by scanning the PayPal QR code at the nursery stand or by using the link below:

https://www.paypal.com/paypalme/natedoesweb

If you’d simply like to support the nursery, you’re welcome to use the same link. Every contribution—whether it’s for a plant or an extra donation—helps us purchase seeds, soil, pots, trays, labels, and other supplies so we can continue growing plants for our neighbors.
